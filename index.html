ABOUT THE DIVISION

Three ideals,
one delta.

The Delta brandmark is built from three parts that only form a whole when combined — the same way Texas FBLA Collegiate members build careers by combining classroom knowledge with real leadership practice.

01

Service

Chapters give back to their campuses and home communities through volunteer projects, financial literacy outreach, and partnerships with local nonprofits.

02

Education

Workshops, speaker series, and certification prep sharpen the business, technology, and communication skills members carry into internships and full-time roles.

03

Progress

Competitive events and officer tracks push members to test their skills against peers statewide and nationally, then bring what they learn back to their chapter.

Led by members, for members.
